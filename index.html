Our Story

About Us

A charity established by members of the Purfleet community to create a dedicated masjid for our growing community.

Our Community

Purfleet Muslim Community and Masjid is a charity established by members of the Purfleet community. Our goal is to create a dedicated masjid to serve the growing and vibrant Muslim community in the Purfleet area.

Our Vision

To build a purpose-built masjid that will serve as a spiritual, educational, and community hub for Muslims in Purfleet and surrounding areas for generations to come.

Future Masjid

Help us build a permanent masjid for the Purfleet community. Your donations will help us achieve this important goal and create a lasting legacy.
